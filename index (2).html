02 — Charity

School Scholarship Fund

A small fund, started by a few young students, with one clear aim: to give five children the education they deserve.

What the funding supports ↓

Our goal 5children
Through Education
Creating Opportunity
Towards Social mobility

In our words

About the
fund

Our charity consists of a few young students who are aiming to fund 5 underprivileged children to enable them the opportunity of a well-deserved education.

We believe education is a critical part of social mobility and can help these children have the futures they deserve.

Our goal is to support 5 children in Pakistan occupied Kashmir, funding their tuition fees and resources such as books and uniforms.

We are supporting the most talented students who shall benefit from the funding.

Many of us have been able to benefit from an education that has enabled us to succeed in life.

We believe every child has the right to get on the path to success.

Why it matters

Why
education

We see education as one of the most important routes to social mobility.

A good education can open doors that would otherwise stay closed — and it can give a child real choices about the future they want. For talented students, cost should not be the thing that holds them back.

Many of us have benefited from an education that has helped us to succeed. This project is our way of helping others onto that same path, by focusing on a small number of children and supporting them properly.

Where the money goes

What the funding supports

  • 01

    Tuition fees

    Covering school fees, so that cost is not the reason a child misses out.

  • 02

    Books

    The books each child needs to learn and keep up in class.

  • 03

    School uniforms

    So every child can attend school ready, and on an equal footing.

  • 04

    Essential resources

    The other everyday educational materials that learning depends on.

Who we support

Five
students

Our goal is to support five children in Pakistan occupied Kashmir.

We are focusing on the most talented students — those who stand to benefit most from the funding — and on covering what they need to stay in school: tuition fees, books, uniforms and essential resources.

Every child has the right to get on the path to success.
