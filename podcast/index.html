About the
podcast

A father and son. Two generations. Different perspectives on the world we live in.

Hosted by Raja Adil Bashir & Shahyaan Adil, we have open, thought-provoking conversations about life, relationships, success, happiness, money, technology, society and the challenges facing different generations today.

Sometimes we agree. Sometimes we see things completely differently.

But the aim is always the same: to question assumptions, understand another point of view, and have the conversations that are worth having.
